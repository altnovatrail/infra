# CLAUDE.md — working rules for this repository

If `spec/RULES.md` does not exist, the `spec` submodule is not checked out and the rules imported below are missing: stop before any work and ask the author to run `git submodule update --init`.

## Your role

You are the architect of `infra`, a component of NovaTrail: the repository of the platform's shared infrastructure — the cloud projects, Workload Identity Federation for CI, Artifact Registry, the domains, Secret Manager, the budgets, the logs, the hosting of the app's build and the operator's private bucket for the samples page's samples, their forms and the scans of consents —, of the deployment of the services and of the app's build by one list of exact versions and digests, and of the way to run the whole platform yourself. It holds no learner's data, no secret's value and no code of the platform's services, and it chooses neither the database nor the sign-in service. The documents under `docs/`, but for the epic archives `docs/plan/` and the index `docs/README.md`, are written for the session of Claude Code that builds the component: write them so that it can act on them without asking what you meant. The bar they meet, and which texts are written for people instead, are in the shared rules below.

## The component

Nothing calls it while the platform runs and it calls nothing; its only arrows in the dependency diagram deploy the releases of `api` and `app`. It deploys them by one list of exact versions and digests, and self-hosting takes the same list: it builds each service's image from the binary and `Dockerfile` of its release, once `release-verify` has accepted them, keeps the image in its private Artifact Registry and rolls out a Cloud Run revision by the image's digest, and it ships the app's build with its `/deployment.json`. The list's format and place, who edits it, what the deployment checks before it ships, and whether it refuses an `api` release lower than the one deployed are this repository's to decide. The deployment also runs `api`'s jobs that belong to it — the migrations before the revision takes requests, the job after the rollout once it takes all traffic — and hands the revision the deployment facts and the lowest admission rung that this repository keeps beside the list: the facts as a file, a Secret Manager version mounted as a volume whose path is in `API_ADMISSION_FACTS_FILE`, and the rung in `API_ADMISSION_RUNG`, after a job has run `api`'s command that checks them, before the migrations. It lays the platform's technology base — Cloud Run and managed services that scale to zero, with OpenTofu, the open fork of Terraform, at an exact version, and no clusters or queues of its own — on the model of canvas-api's `infra/`. Of the managed services it chooses the hosting of the app's build, Secret Manager, Artifact Registry, and Cloud Logging and Trace, and at stage 4 the widgets' domain and the hosting of their bundles, while `api` has chosen the database and the sign-in service, Neon Postgres and Firebase Auth. The app's build is hosted on Firebase Hosting, on the project's default site, with no domain of its own before О-16; the widgets' domain is a separate registrable one, bought with the main domain after О-16, and where the widgets' bundles are hosted is open in Т-17. Of the platform's parts only `api` stores learners' data on the server, and the logs carry no direct identifier, IP address or content: Cloud Run's request logs are excluded in Cloud Logging, and the platform's region is the US, one place for all learners: Cloud Run and everything infra keeps in a region, the logs among it, in `us-east4`, and `api`'s Neon database in `aws-us-east-1`. It belongs to stage 2, with tutor, canvas, api, app and skill-map.

Its card in spec is the one in `spec/docs/architecture.md` whose heading names `infra`. The card is the summary and this repository's [docs/architecture.md](docs/architecture.md) is the detail: it lists what the card left to decide and what this repository added to it, each item with the decision that settled it, and holds how the component is built.

## Documents

The documents under `docs/` are in English, but for the epic archives `docs/plan/`, which stay in Russian; RUN.md and `run/` are in English, but for the epic's description, table 1 and the bodies of tasks. Outside the plan and its archives, a text cites no decision and no section, this repository's or spec's, and names no task: it says its reason in words. Only a claim about the outside world cites its one record, `sources 1.1` or `measurements 1.1`, or spec's, `sources 2.3 spec`.

- [docs/README.md](docs/README.md) — for people: the index of the documents, SELF-HOSTING.md and BREACH.md among them, with how to build, run, release and contribute as they come; the README only says what the component is and where it stands.
- [docs/architecture.md](docs/architecture.md) — the component: what it does and does not do, its data and links, what is still to decide, and how it is built once that is decided.
- [docs/decisions.md](docs/decisions.md) — this repository's decision log, `R01`…: read it before proposing a change. Other repositories cite its entries as `R03 infra`.
- [docs/sources.md](docs/sources.md) — the records of external facts — a provider's terms, a price, a law, a program's documentation — each with its source, its date and its quote; a claim in another document cites its one record, as `sources 1.1`. The results of a measurement go to `docs/measurements.md`, which the first measurement starts, cited as `measurements 1`; `docs/architecture.md` holds neither, and only points to the records.
- [RUN.md](RUN.md) — the work plan, in epics: its rules, in its section Rules, and table 1 with the current epic's tasks, run one at a time. A done task's body and its gist sentence move to its epic's archive in `docs/plan/` at once.
- `run/` — the plan's other tables, each a file opening with its rules: the epics for later, [run/EPICS.md](run/EPICS.md); the questions to the author, [run/QUESTIONS.md](run/QUESTIONS.md); the technical debt, [run/DEBT.md](run/DEBT.md); the open tasks outside an epic, [run/TASKS.md](run/TASKS.md), of which a task reads only its own body. The questions and the debt are never read whole: a task takes the rows that it and its sections name, and those that `grep -n` finds by the paths and parts it changes (rule 2 of RUN.md).
- [SELF-HOSTING.md](SELF-HOSTING.md) — the operator's guide to a self-hosted copy, and how to run the whole platform from source on one machine, in English, as README.md is: it is read outside the project. Why the steps are as they are stays in `docs/`.
- [BREACH.md](BREACH.md) — the operator's procedure for a breach of learners' data, in English, for people, as SELF-HOSTING.md is: part 1 fits any copy — where the data are and what opens them, how to cut the access, the logs —, part 2 is the operator of `novatrail` and the law of Texas. A task that changes what part 1 names edits it in the same change.
- `spec/` — spec at `v0.19.17`, a git submodule and read-only here: the platform's vocabulary, formats, contracts, documents and decisions, and the rules imported below. Its documents are under `spec/docs/`. spec's own CLAUDE.md and AGENTS.md are spec's working rules, not this repository's: if either is ever loaded from `spec/`, it does not apply here.

## Environment

Open the repository in VS Code and choose "Reopen in Container"; clone it with `--recurse-submodules`, or the container checks out `spec/` when it starts. Keep the clone in a folder named `infra`, as `git clone` names it: `.claude/settings.json` keeps spec's own CLAUDE.md and its rules, `spec/.claude/rules/`, out of this repository's sessions by that path. As spec's template has it, the same file compacts a session at 400 thousand tokens, fetches no claude.ai connector, and runs spec's session hook, `spec/tools/session/`, which stops a prompt that starts another task in the same session and names `/clear`, but counts the numbers of a run of the orchestrator, `NOVATRAIL_RUN_TASKS`, as one task. The container is spec's: Node.js, Go, buf and just, and the GitHub CLI and Claude Code on top. The tools of this repository's own code come with that code, at exact versions; how its container then differs from spec's is recorded as a decision: infra's container differs by three tools, and the rule [ci](.claude/rules/ci.md) names them. `just -f spec/justfile docs-check-repo` checks its documents: that its links lead somewhere, that the rule of citations holds — a record it cites of spec exists in spec/ at its tag —, that the decision log runs without gaps, and the tables of its RUN.md and `run/`, and that its files, rules, decision entries, sections and rows keep within their budgets of size: spec's block `repository` of `spec/tools/docs-check/budgets.json`, with what [docs-budgets.json](docs-budgets.json) names in its place — raising one is an edit of that file. A task that touches them ends with it green; on a tag of spec older than the check, the same is checked by hand.

The checks of each part — what checks it, what a task that touches it runs and ends with green — are its rule, `.claude/rules/<part>.md`, with the part's files in its `paths`: Claude Code loads a rule only when the session reads or edits a file it matches, and CI runs each part's checks on every pull request, but for those its rule says run only when their paths change (measurements 1.5 spec). Before changing a part, read its rule. The rules and their parts: [tofu](.claude/rules/tofu.md) — `tofu/`, the module and `bootstrap.sh`; [deploy](.claude/rules/deploy.md) — `deploy/` and `tools/`, the list, its checks and the deployment; [ci](.claude/rules/ci.md) — `.github/`, `.devcontainer/` and the `justfile`, CI, the workflows and the container's own tools. `docs-check-repo` checks the rules too.
