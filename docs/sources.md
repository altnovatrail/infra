# infra — sources

> The external facts the repository's documents under `docs/` rest on: a provider's terms, a price, a law, a standard, a program's documentation. Here are their records — the address, the version or date of the page, the date it was read and a quote in the source's language — and [architecture.md](architecture.md) and the decision log hold the claim and a citation of the record by its number, as a section: `sources 1.1` (RULES.md, The bar; R143 spec; R26).
>
> A record is a subsection "N.M" of a topic: one source, or one reading of several sources for one decision. A record's number never changes: a new one goes at the end of its topic, a new topic at the end of the file. A source read again is an item with its date in the same record, and the earlier text is not edited. The model is sources spec. The results of measurements are not here but in `docs/measurements.md`, which the first measurement starts. The quotes that an entry of the decision log gives itself stay in the entry: the log is not rewritten.
>
> Until T52 the records stood in [architecture.md](architecture.md), section 4, an item per source. T52 moved them word for word, a topic per part of how the component is built, and wrote the citation of a section at the start of an item — `(раздел 2.1)` — as `(architecture 2.1)`: a bare «раздел» here would mean a section of this file. A record's heading is the item's former name, and T53 translated it with the file: an item that the archives of `docs/plan/` name in Russian is found by its heading in this file at commit a2db250, where it is still in Russian, and its record's number is the same (R26).
