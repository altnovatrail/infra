# infra — sources

> The facts of the outside world the repository's documents under `docs/` rest on and no run of ours checks: a law, a regulator, a standard's normative text, a provider's terms and policies, a price, a license, a page that changes in place with no version, a text outside the repositories. Here are their records — the address, the date it was read and a quote in the source's language — and [architecture.md](architecture.md) and the decision log hold the claim and a citation of its one record by its number, `sources 1.1`, not a chain. A record's heading names its topic, not a task.
>
> No record is needed for a library's documentation or code at a version or commit — a link beside the claim —, for a behaviour a test or a measurement checks — they are its evidence —, or for spec's tags and the files of NovaTrail's and MathTrail's repositories — git, at a commit.
>
> A record is a subsection "N.M" of a topic: one source, or one reading of several sources for one decision. A record's number never changes and is never given again: a new one goes at the end of its topic, a new topic at the end of the file, and a record removed leaves a gap. A source read again with the same text changes the record's date; with another text, the claims and decisions on it are rewritten in the same change and the record holds the new reading alone — where a decision waits on the author, an open question, and both readings stand until it is closed. The model is `spec/docs/sources.md`. The results of measurements are not here but in [measurements.md](measurements.md). The quotes that an entry of the decision log gives itself stay in the entry.
>
> A record that the archives of `docs/plan/` name by its former Russian heading is found by that heading in this file at commit a2db250; its number is the same. The archives keep citing a record removed since.

Of the bucket's own permissions the role Owner holds by itself `storage.buckets.create`, `storage.buckets.delete`, `storage.buckets.list`, `storage.buckets.createTagBinding`, `storage.buckets.deleteTagBinding`, `storage.buckets.getIpFilter`, `storage.buckets.listEffectiveTags`, `storage.buckets.listTagBindings` and `storage.buckets.setIpFilter` — not `storage.buckets.get`, `update`, `getIamPolicy` or `setIamPolicy`;
- the same page: `roles/storage.legacyBucketOwner` — "Grants permission to create, overwrite, and delete objects; list objects in a bucket and read object metadata, excluding allow policies, when listing; and read and edit bucket metadata, including allow policies", among its permissions `storage.buckets.get`, `storage.buckets.getIamPolicy`, `storage.buckets.setIamPolicy`, `storage.buckets.update`, `storage.objects.create`, `storage.objects.delete` and `storage.objects.list`, and not `storage.objects.get`; `roles/storage.legacyBucketReader` — "Grants permission to list a bucket's contents and read bucket metadata, excluding allow policies"; `roles/storage.legacyObjectOwner` — "Grants permission to view and edit objects and their metadata, including ACLs", `storage.objects.get` among them; `roles/storage.legacyObjectReader` — "Grants permission to view objects and their metadata, excluding ACLs".

What the page does not say — that a policy without the owners' binding shuts the project's owner out of the bucket, how IAM spells a person's member, and that Owner takes no token of a service account — the project showed: measurements 3.7 and 3.8.
